## Interpretation and next work

This is meaningful progress toward making mathematics somewhere you can go: mathematical content, prepared collision, scale changes and stable reconstruction now work together in a small local example. The failed bright-wall prediction remains a lesson about experiment design, not a reason to replace functioning geometry. Alternative CPU-only specimens are preserved and parked; no alternative world was built or adopted.

The remaining timing gaps are measured optimization work. The visitor is held at a valid pose while preparation completes. Prefetching farther ahead and reducing collision-query tails can be addressed as broader exploration exposes their practical impact. The current measurements do not establish seamless exploration, complete memory ownership, arbitrary joins or client-only generation.

Next is bounded world preparation around actual observer positions, with new-source coverage, stable region identities and bounded residency, carrying this same fixed-world scale behavior forward. Keep the native implementation as the measured control. Browser/client-versus-service architecture remains a separate decision; this experiment does not choose a product default.
